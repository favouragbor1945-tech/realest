Enrollment & Registration

Questions about course registration, prerequisites, and enrollment process

For Level 1 (Fundamentals): Basic computer literacy and enthusiasm for geospatial technology. No prior programming experience required.

For Level 2 (Machine Learning): Completion of Level 1 or equivalent experience with Python basics and GIS concepts.

For Level 3 (Advanced): Completion of Level 2 or professional experience in geospatial analysis.

For first-time students, we require enrollment in Level 1 and Level 2 as they build foundational skills essential for advanced GeoAI work. However, experienced professionals with proven background in geospatial analysis and machine learning may request exemption through our assessment process.

Note: Level 3 can be registered at once with Levels 1 & 2, or separately at a later time if you prefer phased enrollment.

The enrollment process takes 1-3 business days:

  1. Course Selection: Choose your courses on the courses page
  2. Payment: Complete payment via your preferred method
  3. Registration: Receive registration confirmation email
  4. Onboarding: Get access to pre-course materials and setup guide
  5. Start Learning: Begin classes on the scheduled start date

You'll receive all login credentials and platform access within 24 hours of payment confirmation.

Technical Requirements

Questions about system requirements, software, and technical setup

💻 Hardware Requirements

  • Processor: Intel i5 or equivalent (i7 recommended for ML)
  • RAM: 8GB minimum (16GB recommended)
  • Storage: 256GB SSD minimum (512GB+ recommended)
  • Graphics: Dedicated GPU with 4GB+ VRAM (recommended for deep learning)
  • Internet: Stable 10+ Mbps connection

🖥️ Software Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10/11, macOS 10.14+, or Linux
  • Browser: Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, or Safari 14+
  • Python: 3.8+ with Anaconda distribution
  • QGIS: Latest LTR version (free download)
  • Git: For version control and collaboration
Note: We provide detailed setup guides and pre-session technical checks to ensure your system is properly configured.

Level 1 (Fundamentals): No prior programming experience required. We start with Python basics and build from there.

Level 2 (Machine Learning): Basic Python knowledge is required (covered in Level 1 or equivalent experience).

Level 3 (Advanced): Intermediate Python and ML knowledge required.
